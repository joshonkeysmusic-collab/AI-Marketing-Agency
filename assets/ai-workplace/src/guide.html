Three questions for every task

List the tasks your team repeats each week. Run each one through these questions, top to bottom.

Does it follow the same steps every time?Could you write the rules on one page?
Yes →Automate
No ↓
Would a good first draft save real time?Emails, summaries, research, replies
Yes →Assist
No ↓
Does it need judgement, trust or a relationship?Pricing, hiring, hard conversations
Yes →Keep human

Rule of thumb: if a mistake would reach a client, keep a person approving it, even when AI does the work.

Step 2 · Start small
3 / 3

Your first 30 days

Week 1Map

List every repeated task. Sort each into Automate, Assist or Keep human. No tools yet.

Week 2Pick one

Choose the Automate task that happens most often and annoys people most. Just one.

Week 3Build

Automate it end to end, with a person approving anything that reaches a client.

Week 4Measure

Compare time spent before and after. Keep what works, then pick the next task.
