**HOOK**
Most companies are asking "which AI tool should we use?"

That's the wrong first question.

**BODY**
The better question is: which work should AI touch at all?

Before I build anything, I sort every repeated task into one of three lanes:

→ Automate
Repetitive, rule-based work. Data entry between tools, follow-up reminders, scheduling, weekly reports.

→ Assist
AI drafts or sorts, a person checks and decides. First drafts, research summaries, lead scoring, routine replies.

→ Keep human
Judgement, trust and relationships. Pricing, hiring, difficult conversations.

Then I start at the top. One Automate task, built end to end, with a person approving anything that reaches a client.

AI takes the admin. People keep the judgement.

That's how AI in the workplace actually sticks.
