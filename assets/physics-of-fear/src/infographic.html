Research on energy, psychology and accountability points to a very different risk than the one in the headlines.

The real risk

Accountability, not apocalypse

An algorithm can't be sued, can't apologise, can't carry blame. Only a human can. The danger isn't AI seizing judgement — it's people quietly handing judgement over, one convenient decision at a time, to something that can never own the outcome.

The real question isn't "will AI take over?"
It's "what am I quietly letting it decide" that only I should?
