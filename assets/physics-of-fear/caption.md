**HOOK**
We're afraid of the wrong AI apocalypse.

**BODY**
The physics say the sci-fi version can't happen yet.

Your brain runs on 12 to 25 watts. Silicon AI hardware needs megawatts to gigawatts, and researchers estimate it's roughly 2.7x less energy-efficient than biological computing (Blue Brain Project data).

One published model, the Erasi equation, puts a number on it: an AI system that could out-think collective humanity would need up to 10 billion times the entire USA's power output.

That's not a software limit. That's thermodynamics.

And yet, surveyed on an AI-dominated future:
→ 96% fear an uncontrollable AI takeover
→ 86% fear an uncontrollable fate
→ 93% fear a meaningless future

We're bracing for Skynet.

Meanwhile, the real risk is already here, and it's quieter.

An algorithm can't be sued. Can't apologise. Can't carry blame.

Only a human can.

The danger isn't AI seizing judgement. It's people handing judgement over, one convenient decision at a time, to something that can never own the outcome.

**TAKEAWAY**
The real question isn't "will AI take over?"

It's "what am I quietly letting it decide, that only I should?"
