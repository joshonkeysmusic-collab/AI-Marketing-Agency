## 2. Gaps (3Ps + Market Gap)

### G1 — "Your CRM is WhatsApp" — score 18/20
- **Problem:** Enquiries, orders and follow-ups live in staff WhatsApp chats and personal phones. When someone is off, the business forgets.
- **People:** Nigerian/African SME owners and ops leads with sales run through WhatsApp. Trigger: a lost order or a customer complaining "I already sent this".
- **Promise:** Keep customers on WhatsApp but give the business memory: every message captured, tagged and turned into a CRM record and task.
- **Market Gap:** Automation advice assumes email + web forms and says "move to a CRM". Nobody shows how to automate *around* WhatsApp-first customers. That's a pan-African gap, and it's Josh's lived experience (the Dubai → Nigeria post).
- **Proof:** Hands-on CRM + messaging-channel integration builds; lived experience in Nigerian businesses.
- **Visual:** architecture diagram, WhatsApp → n8n → AI tagger → CRM + task → shared inbox.
- Scores: pain 5 · intent 5 · gap 5 · proof 3.

### G2 — "Put a number on manual work" — score 16/20
- **Problem:** Founders hear "automation saves time" but can't justify spend; there's no number to compare with the build cost.
- **People:** Founders/owners deciding whether to invest. Trigger: a quote for an automation project, or hiring another admin.
- **Promise:** A 4-step worksheet that turns one manual task into a weekly cost and a payback period.
- **Market Gap:** ROI content is enterprise (vendor playbooks) or vague ("save hours!"). No simple SME-level calculation.
- **Proof:** Formula-based; no claims needed. The worked example is labelled illustrative.
- **Visual:** 9-slide carousel worksheet.
- Scores: pain 4 · intent 5 · gap 4 · proof 3.

### G3 — "Autonomy is earned: the approval gate" — score 15/20
- **Problem:** Owners are nervous about AI agents acting on their behalf: wrong message, wrong quote, reputational damage. So they either avoid agents or over-trust them.
- **People:** Founders who've tried ChatGPT and are curious about agents but risk-averse. Trigger: hearing an "AI agent gone wrong" story, or a pitch for "fully autonomous" tools.
- **Promise:** A design pattern that gets AI speed without losing control, and widens autonomy only as results prove it.
- **Market Gap:** The market is split between hype ("fully autonomous agents") and corporate governance talk. Nothing practical for SMEs in between.
- **Proof:** Josh's own 3-agent LinkedIn pipeline runs on exactly this pattern.
- **Visual:** system schema, agent → proposed action → approval gate → act / reject → log → widen rules.
- Scores: pain 4 · intent 3 · gap 4 · proof 4.
