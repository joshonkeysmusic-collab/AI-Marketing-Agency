# Engagement playbook (shaped by the CV, 23 Sep 2026)

Goal: make Josh visible to founders and operations leads in the **UK, Europe and USA** as the person who **builds** AI systems (CRM, WhatsApp, voice agents, dashboards), not someone who just talks about AI. Every action is drafted by the Creator Agent and **approved by the owner before it's sent**.

## 1. Who to follow (and why)

Follow = see their posts in the feed, so there's something worth commenting on. Buyers first, peers second, tools last.

| Group | Share of follows | Examples / how to find | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A. UK & Europe buyers** | 40% | Founders/COOs of clinics, med spas and aesthetics, wellness, professional services, property, e-commerce in the UK and Europe (search: "founder aesthetics clinic London", "COO scale-up UK", "Head of Operations Berlin") | Josh's strongest proof (med spa AI CRM, skin-analysis engine) is in **aesthetics and wellness**, a big, fragmented market in the UK and Europe |
| **B. US buyers** | 25% | Founders/COOs of med spas, clinics, agencies and home-services businesses in the US (med spas are a large US sector) | Highest budgets; voice agents and AI receptionists are a mature buying category there |
| **C. Ecosystem amplifiers** | 15% | UK/EU/US founder communities and publications: Sifted, Tech Nation alumni, SaaStr, Indie Hackers, med spa and aesthetics associations (AmSpa in the US) | Their posts collect founder comments, so a sharp comment gets seen by the right people |
| **D. Peer builders / creators** | 15% | AI-agent educators (e.g. Rakesh Gohel, AI agent governance), n8n and voice-AI builders | Learn formats and trends; comment as a fellow practitioner. **Don't** chase their audiences, who are mostly other builders, not buyers |
| **E. Tools** | 5% | n8n, Supabase, VAPI, Anthropic / Claude, OpenAI | Product news gives timely post angles ("what this release means for SMEs") |

Target: follow about 15 new accounts a week (mostly A and B), and unfollow accounts whose posts never earn a comment.

## 2. Kinds of comments to leave

Each comment should show that Josh has *built* something related. Use only proof from `proof-library.md`, **20–30 words max**, human-sounding, no links, no pitch, no "Great post!".

| Type | When | Pattern | Example seed from the CV |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Builder's add-on** | A founder or ops lead describes a problem | Agree → name the specific mechanism that fixes it → one detail only a builder would know | "…in the med spa CRM I built, the leak wasn't lead capture, it was *after* the consultation. Automating the WhatsApp follow-up on treatment plans was the fix." |
| **2. Respectful contrarian** | A hype post ("fully autonomous agents!") | Credit the idea → add the missing constraint → practical alternative | Approval gates; "start with one workflow" |
| **3. Dubai ⇄ London view** | Posts about how businesses run, scale or adopt AI | Share an outside-in comparison from working in both cities | What Dubai businesses automate by default that UK/European SMEs still do by hand |
| **4. Question that invites the author back** | Posts with few comments | One sharp, specific question | "When your clinic scaled to a second location, was it bookings or follow-ups that broke first?" |
| **5. Tool-translator** | Tool/product news (n8n, VAPI, Claude, Supabase) | What it means for a non-technical business owner, in one practical use | "For clinics, this makes a VAPI receptionist that books into the CRM a same-week build, not a quarter-long project." |

**Never:** pitch or mention "DM me" in comments; comment on ads; repeat the same comment; quote numbers not in the proof library.

## 3. Daily routine (~20 min, weekdays)
1. **3 comments**: at least 2 on group A/B posts, from the types above
2. **Connection requests (max 15 a day in total, so the weekly count stays well inside LinkedIn's invitation limit):**
   - **5–10 similar accounts with 5,000+ followers** *(added 23 Sep 2026)*: AI automation, AI agents, no-code/n8n, operations and founder-education creators whose audience overlaps Josh's (UK, Europe, USA). **Connect, don't follow.** Many large accounts show "Follow" as the main button, so use the custom-invite link to send a real connection request. Always add a short personal note that references something specific they posted (≤ 200 characters, no pitch).
   - **The rest: buyers** (groups A and B), UK/Europe about 60%, USA about 40%; notes only for high-fit people. Time comments for when each audience is online (UK mornings, US East Coast mornings = UK early afternoon)
3. **Reply to every comment** on Josh's own posts within 2 hours (the Scheduler & Analytics Agent flags them)
4. **Accepted connections**: no pitch. Only if they engage twice, send a short "thanks for the thoughtful comment" message *(owner-approved)*

## 4. Tracking (Scheduler & Analytics Agent)
Acceptance rate by group · comments that got a reply from the author · profile views after comment days · "WORKFLOW" DMs (owner reports).

## 5. Lead-magnet delivery ("Comment WORKFLOW and I'll send you the PDF")
Runs only on posts where the full asset is **locked** (not attached). Owner: Engagement Manager.

1. **Watch**: twice a day (late morning, late afternoon UK time), list everyone who commented the keyword or DM'd it.
2. **Check connection status** for each person:
   - **Connected** → draft a DM with the PDF attached.
   - **Not connected** → LinkedIn only allows messages to connections (or paid InMail), so draft a **connection request with a note**: "Thanks for commenting on my AI guide post. Connect and I'll send the PDF straight over." Send the PDF once they accept.
3. **Josh approves the batch** (one list per run: name, connected yes/no, message). Nothing is sent before that.
4. **Send**, then reply under their comment **after** the DM has gone: "Sent, {first name}. Check your messages." Never say "sent" before it is.
5. **Log the lead** in `leads/lead-magnets.csv`: name, headline, company, post, keyword, date sent, replied (y/n).
6. **One follow-up only**, 3–4 days later, and only if they haven't replied. Owner-approved, no pitch: "Did the guide help? Happy to point out the first task I'd automate for you if useful."

**DM template** (short, human, edit per person):
> Hi {first name}, thanks for commenting! Here's the 3-page guide on putting AI where it belongs. If you'd like me to look at which task you'd automate first, just reply here. Josh

Rules: deliver within the same working day; never mass-send identical messages (personalise the first line); stop immediately if someone asks not to be messaged.
