## Risk verdict
- **Hard pivot: moderate-to-high risk.** The algorithm's interest graph is trained on Afrobeats/piano; B2B content will get weak signals and suppressed reach. Follower geography likely Nigeria/Dubai music scene vs a UK/EU/US buyer target.

- **Chosen path: staged bridge on the existing profile.** The warm SMB owners already following are literal ICP; a story-first pivot keeps them, a whiplash rebrand loses everyone.
