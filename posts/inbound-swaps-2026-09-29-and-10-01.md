## Tue 29 Sep, 09:30 UK — framework post (visual: 5-question checklist card, to build after approval)
gap: automation triage · cta_type: dm ("WORKFLOW") · no invented numbers

Should you automate it? My 5-question test.

Most people start with "which tool?" I start here:

1. Does it happen more than once a week?
2. Are the steps the same every time?
3. Is a mistake cheap and easy to undo?
4. Is the information already digital?
5. Does a human need to decide, or only to remember?

Yes to 1 to 4, and "only remember" on 5? Automate it.

If a human needs to decide, automate the prep and keep the decision.

That last rule is why nothing in my own setup sends without a tap.

## Thu 1 Oct, 09:30 UK — proof post (real event from this week; visual: 3-check card, to build after approval)
gap: trust in agents · cta_type: dm ("WORKFLOW")

My AI agent refused to work this week.

I asked it to run my LinkedIn engagement. It opened the browser, checked whose account was signed in, and found it wasn't mine.

It stopped and told me, instead of carrying on.

That's why I build checks into every agent:

1. Verify identity before acting
2. Draft first, send only after approval
3. Log every action so it can be reviewed

An agent that doesn't check who it's acting as is one wrong login away from posting under the wrong name.

Speed is easy. Trust is the part worth building.
