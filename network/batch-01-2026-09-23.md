## B. Comments (3)

**C1: EUGE Digital Technologies** (5 d, survey on how businesses manage WhatsApp, Instagram, enquiries and follow-ups). Fits G1.
> Listening before building is the right call. The pattern I see most with Nigerian SMEs: WhatsApp isn't just a channel, it *is* the CRM. Enquiries, orders and follow-ups all live in staff phones. Any platform that makes businesses leave WhatsApp will struggle. The ones that capture what's already happening there will win. Curious whether your survey shows the same.

**C2: Mohammed Murtala Okadu** (1 w, "Most logistics SMEs do not have a people problem. They have a repetitive-process problem.")
> Point 1 is where I'd start too. In most SMEs I've worked with, the order doesn't arrive through a form. It arrives as a WhatsApp voice note or a phone call. Getting that into a structured job record automatically unlocks the other four. "Standardise, then automate" is the right order.

**C3: Jerry Doubles** (1 d, AI Operating Systems for African SMEs). Comment on the idea, deliberately without repeating his statistics.
> Agreed that the gap is implementation, not access to tools. I'd add one thing for SMEs: start with one workflow and a human approval step before anything goes to a customer. Trust gets built one automated decision at a time, not with one big system switched on overnight.
