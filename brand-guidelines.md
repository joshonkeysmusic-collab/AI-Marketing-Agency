## Pillar 2 — Messaging Strategy

### Content themes (the only five the Creator Agent may use)
| ID | Theme | Example angle |
|---|---|---|
| T1 | AI automation how-tos | "How to auto-qualify inbound leads in 5 steps" |
| T2 | Multi-agent architecture | "The 3-agent system running my LinkedIn (and why it's split this way)" |
| T3 | n8n workflows | "The n8n workflow that replaced 6 hours of weekly admin" |
| T4 | Business case studies / ROI | "Before vs after: what automation changed for an SME's operations" |
| T5 | Lessons from building | "What broke in my first agent build — and the fix" |
| T6 | Voice & chat agents *(added from CV)* | "The AI receptionist that actually books into your CRM" |
| T7 | AI dashboards & decision systems *(added from CV)* | "What → Why → How: dashboards that tell you what to do" |

**Priority verticals (from proof):** clinics, med spas and wellness (strongest proof, big in Dubai) · real estate and rentals · logistics and mobility.

### Post-type mix
| Type | Share | Per month (16 posts) |
|---|---|---|
| Practical teaching / tutorials | 40% | 6–7 |
| Personal case studies / behind-the-scenes | 25% | 4 |
| Expert opinions / industry insights | 20% | 3 |
| Direct offers / CTAs | 15% | 2–3 |

### Voice rules
- **First person ("I").** Professional yet accessible — explain as if to a smart founder who isn't technical.
- **UK English:** optimise, organise, colour, behaviour, programme (non-software), analyse. Dates as 23 September 2026.
- Short sentences. One idea per line. Plain words over jargon; if a technical term is needed (MCP, webhook, agent), define it in ≤ 10 words the first time.
- Local context from London or Dubai is welcome **when relevant**, written for a UK/European/US reader. Avoid currency symbols in examples, or use £/€/$ consistently for the audience.

### Hard rules (Creator Agent must refuse to draft otherwise)
- ❌ No politics.
- ❌ No unverified claims. Any number (time saved, % uplift, revenue) must be either from the owner's own build with the owner's confirmation, or cited to a named source.
- ❌ No confidential client data, names, or screenshots without written permission from the client. Default to anonymised ("a med spa", "a logistics company").
- ❌ No income promises or financial advice (applies especially to the secondary theme).
- ❌ No disparaging named competitors or individuals.

### Calls to action (CTA library, updated 23 Sep 2026)
Exactly **one CTA per post**. The Content Creator picks it per post; the Posting Manager checks it matches how the post is published.

| CTA | Wording (variants allowed) | Use when |
|---|---|---|
| **DM** (default) | "DM me 'WORKFLOW' and I'll show you what I'd automate first in your business." | Offer posts, case studies, most teaching posts |
| **Comment-to-get** (lead magnet) | "Comment 'WORKFLOW' and I'll send you the full [3-page guide]." | **Only when the full asset is locked**, i.e. *not* attached to the post. The post shows a teaser (infographic, image or page 1), and the full PDF is sent by DM |
| **Follow** | "Follow Josh Forkwa for more AI automation breakdowns." | Build-in-public, opinion posts |
| **Save** | "Save this for your next 'should we automate this?' conversation." | When the full asset *is* attached (a carousel or PDF people keep) |

- **Keyword:** `WORKFLOW` by default. A campaign may use its own keyword (e.g. `GUIDE`) so leads can be traced to one asset; log it in the post file.
- **Never** use comment-to-get when the PDF is already attached. People would comment for something they already have, which reads as engagement bait.
- Every lead-magnet DM is drafted by the Engagement Manager and **sent only after Josh approves the batch** (see `strategy/engagement-playbook.md` §5).
- Never put two CTAs in one post.

### Post anatomy (text posts)
1. **Hook** (line 1–2, ≤ 210 characters so it shows before "…see more"): bold claim, surprising number, or a specific outcome.
2. **Tension:** the problem in the reader's words.
3. **Body:** steps / architecture / lesson — scannable, spaced lines.
4. **Takeaway:** one sentence they can remember.
5. **CTA:** one, from the list above.
6. **Hashtags:** 3–5, on the final line.

## Pillar 3 — Visual Assets

### Mood
Minimal and clean with a high-tech edge: **dark background, crisp high-contrast type, one signature accent colour.** Space does the work — no clutter, no stock-photo robots.

### Colour palette
| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `bg` | `#0B0F14` | Main background (near-black, slight blue) |
| `surface` | `#141B24` | Cards, panels, diagram nodes |
| `border` | `#243040` | Dividers, node outlines |
| `text` | `#F2F5F8` | Headlines and body |
| `text-muted` | `#8A97A6` | Captions, labels, page numbers |
| `accent` (signature) | `#19E6A1` | **"Signal Green"** — key words, arrows, highlights, CTA buttons |
| `accent-2` | `#4C8DFF` | Secondary diagram flows only (never competes with accent on the same element) |
| `warn` | `#FFB547` | "Before / problem" states in comparisons |

Rules: accent covers **≤ 10%** of any design. Never place `text-muted` on `surface` for body copy (use `text`). Light-background variants are not part of the brand.

### Typography
| Role | Font | Weight | Fallback (Canva) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Headlines | **Space Grotesk** | 700 | Montserrat Bold |
| Body | **Inter** | 400 / 500 | Open Sans |
| Code, node names, numbers | **JetBrains Mono** | 500 | Space Mono |

Carousel scale (1080 px wide): H1 72–88 px · H2 48–56 px · body 32–36 px · label 24 px. Max 25 words per carousel slide body.

### Face / personal branding
- **Banner:** yes, portrait cut-out on the right third, on `bg`.
- **Carousels:** face on the **final slide only** (CTA slide), circular crop 280 px with a 4 px `accent` ring.
- **Key posts:** personal case studies and offer posts may use a photo; teaching posts use diagrams.
- Photo style: well-lit, neutral or dark background, confident and approachable, no sunglasses/filters.

### Graphic elements
- **Diagrams:** rounded rectangles (radius 16 px) on `surface`, 2 px `border` outline, `accent` arrows, labels in JetBrains Mono.
- **Icons:** outline style, 2 px stroke, `text` or `accent` only.
- **Signature motif:** a thin `accent` line/"signal" connecting nodes — reuse it across banner, carousels, and diagrams.
- ❌ No generic AI imagery (glowing brains, humanoid robots, blue binary rain).

## Pillar 4 — Display Guidelines

### Formats and specs
| Format | Size | Rules |
|---|---|---|
| Text post | ≤ 3,000 chars (target 900–1,500) | Blank line between every 1–2 lines; bullets as `→` or `•`; hook ≤ 210 chars |
| Carousel (PDF) | 1080 × 1350 px (4:5), 6–10 slides | Slide 1 hook · slides 2–n one idea each · last slide face + CTA · slide number bottom-right in `text-muted` · handle `@Josh Forkwa` bottom-left |
| Architecture / diagram image | 1080 × 1350 px (feed) or 1200 × 627 px (link-style) | Title top-left, max ~7 nodes, flow left→right or top→bottom |
| Single image | 1080 × 1350 px | Text on image ≤ 12 words |

### Publishing cadence
- *Updated 23 Sep 2026 (owner instruction):* cadence is **set by the Creator Agent's strategic loop** (see `creator-agent.md`) within guardrails of **2–5 posts per week**, in two audience windows (UK time): **07:30–10:00** (UK and Europe mornings) and **13:00–15:00** (US East Coast mornings). Every change is reported to the owner with its reason.
- Default when there's no data yet: 4 posts a week, Mon 08:30 · Tue 09:00 · Wed 08:30 · Thu 09:00.
- Formats are chosen per market gap, not per weekday.
- **Every post requires the account owner's explicit approval before it is published.** The Scheduler Agent queues and prepares; the owner confirms each publish.

### Emoji and hashtags
- **Light emoji:** max 3 per post, functional only (→ ✅ ⚙️ 📌). None in the hook's first 5 words.
- **Hashtags:** 3–5 per post, last line. Rotate from:
  `#AIAutomation #AIAgents #n8n #BusinessAutomation #WorkflowAutomation #DigitalTransformation #SMEGrowth #MultiAgentSystems #NoCode`

### Profile page
**Banner (1584 × 396 px):** `bg` background; left: one-line promise in Space Grotesk — *"AI agents & automation that give businesses their time back."*; below it in `text-muted`: *"n8n · MCP · Multi-agent systems"*; right: portrait cut-out; `accent` signal line motif linking the two. Keep key content out of the bottom-left 400 px (covered by profile photo on desktop) and centred for mobile crop.

**Headline formula (≤ 220 chars):**
`I help [audience] [outcome] with [method] | [role @ company] | [proof/specialism]`
Draft:
> I help businesses automate operations with AI agents & workflows | Digital Systems & Automation Lead @ Digital Core Systems | n8n · MCP · Multi-agent builds

**About section (≤ 2,600 chars) structure:**
1. Hook — the problem founders feel (manual work eating growth).
2. What I do — positioning statement.
3. How — agents, n8n workflows, MCP integrations; 3 example outcomes (verified only).
4. Who it's for — founders, SME owners, operations leads in the UK, Europe and USA.
5. Credentials — Digital Core Systems, Tropics Institute, builds.
6. CTA — "DM me to discuss automating your business workflows."

**Featured section (3 items, in order):**
1. Best-performing carousel (update monthly from analytics).
2. "How I work / services" post or document with the DM CTA.
3. A multi-agent architecture breakdown (e.g. this LinkedIn pipeline).
